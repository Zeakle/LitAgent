# Survey Review Checklist

## What to check
1. **Coverage** — are seminal papers included? major approaches represented?
2. **Citation accuracy** — does every cited paper exist in the source material?
3. **Internal consistency** — any contradicting quantitative claims (same model, different numbers)?
4. **Fair comparison** — are methods compared under comparable settings?
5. **Structure** — logical taxonomy? clear sections?

## Scoring guidance
- 0.9+: comprehensive, accurate, well-structured
- 0.7-0.8: solid but minor gaps or unclear sections
- <0.7: missing key work, unclear, or has factual issues
Return score + verdict (accept/revise) + specific weaknesses.
