# Survey Writing Methodology

## Structure
1. **Introduction & motivation** — why this field matters, scope
2. **Taxonomy** — group approaches into coherent categories (by method family, not chronology)
3. **Detailed analysis** — per category, cover key methods, their tradeoffs
4. **Experimental comparison** — benchmark tables, note dataset/setting differences
5. **Open problems** — gaps, future directions

## Principles
- Group by conceptual family, not paper-by-paper
- Every claim needs a citation to a real source paper
- Compare fairly: note when methods use different backbones/datasets (not directly comparable)
- Prefer synthesis (what the field learned) over summary (what each paper did)
